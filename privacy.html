Mindflower STEP Mail

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Mindflower is operated by the owner of the dedicated Gmail account used for STEP messages. The integration requests read-only access so the owner can monitor official U.S. Department of State STEP mail.

Data accessed and use

When enabled by the owner, the integration reads message IDs, Gmail receipt timestamps, and raw message content from the dedicated mailbox. It stores raw mail encrypted in the owner's protected server database. It uses this data to identify service mail, verify message origin, and examine possible official updates. It does not send, modify, label, delete, or unsubscribe from messages. The production Gmail connection is currently disabled.

Messages may contain personal information, links, or one-time codes. The system keeps raw message content out of application logs and diagnostic reports. The production AI feature is currently disabled; raw Gmail messages are not sent to an AI provider.

Sharing, storage, and deletion

If the owner enables Gmail delivery, the bot may send a labelled, redacted excerpt from a relevant official bulletin to the owner's private Telegram chat or to subscribers who explicitly opted in for that location. It removes detected email addresses, web links, and six-to-eight-digit codes from the excerpt; other contextual details may remain. Telegram delivers these excerpts and processes the resulting messages under its own terms. The bot filters service mail and mail from senders outside its STEP allowlist.

Raw messages and complete derived copies in staging, databases, and encrypted backups are retained for no more than 30 days from first capture by the production system. Limited research metadata may remain without an indefinite copy of the original message. Gmail messages in the mailbox are not deleted by Mindflower. Existing local pilot material is kept separate from the production database.

The owner can revoke Gmail access in Google Account settings. Revocation stops future reads but does not itself remove already captured encrypted copies; the 30-day retention process applies to those copies.

Scope and security

The only requested Google scope is https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly. OAuth credentials and the raw-mail encryption key are stored outside the source repository with restricted file permissions. Access is limited to one dedicated mailbox.

Questions can be sent to the developer contact shown on the Google OAuth consent screen.
