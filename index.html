Mindflower STEP Mail

Mindflower is a private research tool operated by its owner. It monitors official public travel and aviation notices. Its optional STEP Mail connection reads messages in one dedicated Gmail account to capture U.S. Department of State STEP updates.

The Gmail connection requests gmail.readonly. It cannot send, delete, label, or modify email. An incoming STEP message is checked for origin, content, country, and action before it can be considered as an alert. The current Gmail pilot is local and does not publish email-derived alerts.

Access is limited to the owner of the dedicated mailbox. This is not a public Gmail integration or a service for other users.
