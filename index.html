Mindflower STEP Mail

Mindflower is a private research tool operated by its owner. It monitors official public travel and aviation notices. Its optional STEP Mail connection reads messages in one dedicated Gmail account to capture U.S. Department of State STEP updates.

The Gmail connection requests gmail.readonly. It cannot send, delete, label, or modify email. A message is not treated as a confirmed diplomatic measure merely because it arrived from STEP: the software checks its origin, content, country, and action separately. The production bot is private; its Gmail connection is currently disabled. After the owner connects it, the bot may deliver a clearly labelled, redacted source excerpt to the owner or to Telegram users who explicitly opted in for the relevant location.

Only the owner can authorize access to the dedicated Gmail mailbox. Telegram subscribers cannot access the mailbox or its raw messages. This is not a public Gmail integration for other people's accounts.
